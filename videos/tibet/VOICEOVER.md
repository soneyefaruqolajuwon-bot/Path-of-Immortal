## Fact fixes made to the original draft
| Original | Fixed | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Himalayas "tower even higher into the stratosphere" | "tower far higher still" | Everest (~8,850 m) is in the troposphere, below the stratosphere. |
| Plateau "higher than the tallest peaks in the continental United States" | "...tallest mountain in the lower forty-eight United States" | Denali (Alaska) is far taller; Mt Whitney (~4,420 m) is just under the plateau's ~4,500 m average. |
| "isn't politics or secret military bases" | "isn't MAINLY politics..." | China does restrict parts of its airspace; physics is the main reason. |
| "diversion airports are hours away" | "can be very far away" | Overstated. |
| jet fuel "can thicken into a slush" | crews watch fuel temperature and change altitude/route | Cold fuel is a managed risk, not routine slush. |
| Lhasa jets have "upgraded engines" | specially equipped: extra oxygen, special engine/performance settings, trained pilots | More accurate. |
| Title "Why No Commercial Airlines Fly Over Tibet" | "Why Airlines Avoid Flying Over Tibet" | A few routes do cross with special procedures. |
