intentional). Send me the mp3 and I'll speed it up 1.2x and sync it (`python tools/align_vo.py take.mp3 && python tools/render.py`).

## What changed from the draft script

| Line | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "in under two seconds" → "within SECONDS" | The "under two seconds" figure has no solid source. A marketplace buyer may check a claim like that, and "within seconds" makes the same point without inventing a statistic. |
| 4 | "2D" → "two-D" | So ElevenLabs reads it as "two dee". |
