# EcomRolodex entry — ElevenLabs voiceover

Calm, confident, premium English voice (Douglas works well). One take, paste as-is. Send the mp3 and it gets the house
1.2x speed-up and is synced to the cuts (`python tools/align_vo.py take.mp3 && python tools/render.py`).

```
[confident] Most Shopify stores look EXACTLY the same.

[beat] Yours shouldn't.

[smooth] A custom homepage… built for mobile.

Product pages… that SELL.

And a cart that feels smooth… from the very first tap.

[warm] Want yours redesigned? Comment… REDESIGN.
```

Rules from the brief this script follows: no invented numbers or order counts, no emojis, no mention of AI, ends on
the "Comment REDESIGN" call to action.
