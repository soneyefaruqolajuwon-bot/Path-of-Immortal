## Fact fixes made to the original draft
| Original | Fixed | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "fourteen Western nations" | "fourteen countries" | The Berlin Conference (1884–85) included the Ottoman Empire and the United States. |

| "Every single government knew…" | "Most knew…" | Overgeneralisation. |
| OAU "made a pact: every nation would respect…" | "a year after founding the OAU, nearly every member…" | OAU founded 1963 (Addis Ababa); border pledge at the 1964 Cairo summit; Somalia and Morocco rejected it. |
| "prevented dozens of catastrophic border wars" | "helped prevent many border wars, though not all" | Unprovable; e.g. Ethiopia–Somalia 1977, Ethiopia–Eritrea 1998. |
